> nobody writes blog posts about.

## Why Spotter exists

Every nutrition app on the App Store fails at the same thing: friction.
Logging a meal takes too long, the database is incomplete, and the
results are barely better than guessing. Adherence dies within weeks.
Mine did, twice.

In parallel, every iPhone Pro since 2020 ships with a LiDAR sensor that
almost nobody uses. The geometric information needed to estimate portion
volume from a photo has been sitting in everyone&apos;s pocket for years. I
wanted to know if the consumer UX could finally catch up to the hardware.

[Spotter](/projects/spotter) is the answer I built. Point the camera at
a plate, get back macros in under three seconds. No typing, no searching,
no barcode scanning.

## For other student builders

The most common failure mode I see in student projects is treating
shipping as a final step. It is the only step that matters.
Everything before it is just preparation. Pick a small enough scope that
you can ship, then ship, then expand.

If you&apos;re reading this and you have a project you&apos;ve been &quot;almost ready
to launch&quot; for more than three months: launch the worse version.
