## Overview

My undergraduate thesis at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid. Where
[Spotter](/projects/spotter) is the consumer product, the TFG is the
research artifact behind a more advanced portion-estimation pipeline,
using the Spotter iOS app as the data-capture and evaluation interface.

The goal: take the consumer-grade single-scan flow and push portion-volume
accuracy closer to research-grade, with explicit error bars per scan and
a reproducible evaluation harness.

## Research questions

1. How much portion-volume accuracy can a single iPhone LiDAR scan
   deliver, vs. lab-grade depth setups?
2. What's the right division of labor between geometric estimation
   (from depth) and semantic reasoning (from multimodal LLMs)?
3. Can the system produce calibrated confidence intervals, not just point
   estimates?
