## Overview

AISC Madrid is the **first AISC chapter in Europe** and the **first
AI student association at UC3M**. We were founded on **1 September
2025** and grew from zero to a community of **500+ people**, run by a
core team of **16**, in our first year. Today we are the most active
student association at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid.

Our mission is simple: make AI feel less like magic. We explain the
fundamentals, the actual tools used in industry day-to-day, and we
bring back alumni and professionals from the companies that are
shaping the field to share how they got there.

## Why this matters

Madrid has world-class engineering schools and a fast-growing AI
hiring market, but the two ecosystems were not talking to each
other. AISC Madrid is the bridge — the place where students learn
the fundamentals, see the real tools that the industry uses, and
meet the professionals who are shaping the field at companies like
Microsoft, GitHub, Hugging Face, and Spotify.

Spain produces strong engineering talent and exports too much of it.
AISC is a small contribution to the inverse — making the local
ecosystem worth staying for.

## What I&apos;d do differently

The strongest signal we have found is that we are a builder
community first and a speaker community second. Speaker events fill
rooms and look good in photos; workshops where members ship
something small produce people who can teach the next cohort. If I
were starting over, I would weight that ratio harder toward
workshops from day one.
