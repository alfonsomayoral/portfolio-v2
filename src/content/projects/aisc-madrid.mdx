## Overview

Madrid has world-class engineering schools and a fast-growing AI hiring
market, but no student-led community connecting the two. AISC Madrid was
founded in July 2025 to be the default first stop for any undergrad in
Madrid who wants to learn, build, or work in AI.

It is the **first AISC chapter in Europe** and the **first AI student
association at UC3M**.

## Why this matters

Spotter taught me to ship; Suntory taught me to ship at scale; AISC
taught me that the best leverage on the AI ecosystem in a country is the
next wave of builders. Spain produces strong engineering talent and
exports too much of it. AISC is a small contribution to the inverse —
making the local ecosystem worth staying for.
