## Overview

Spotter AI is the fitness app I wished existed. Development started in
June 2025 and we shipped to the App Store in February 2026. It runs on
iOS and Android, and it bundles four products that other apps usually
sell separately:

1. **Nutrition with AI.** Take a photo of your plate and get back macros
   (calories, protein, carbs, fat) in seconds. GPT-4o Vision does the
   identification and portion reasoning. Barcode scanner with
   OpenFoodFacts for packaged foods. Health score 0-10 per item.
2. **Workout.** Live gym sessions with sets, reps, weight, and RPE.
   Brzycki 1RM tracking, 500+ exercise library with videos, cardio,
   rest timer that works in background. Coach-student relationships
   for Premium users.
3. **Social.** A feed for sharing completed workouts and meals. Likes,
   comments, follows, public routines. Workout summaries auto-render as
   shareable videos through Remotion.
4. **Streaks and gamification.** 60+ achievement badges, weekly
   challenges, XP-based leagues, daily streaks with a frozen-day grace
   period, a bear mascot called Spotty.

Spotter and the [RGB-D nutritional pipeline of my undergraduate
thesis](/projects/tfg) are independent projects. Spotter uses pretrained
foundation models (GPT-4o, trained on huge food and nutrition data)
served via API. The TFG is the academic research line — custom
architectures with iPhone LiDAR depth, mesh-based volume estimation,
and end-to-end evaluation.

## Tech stack

- **Mobile**: React Native 0.81, Expo SDK 54, Expo Router (file-based),
  TypeScript strict, Zustand state, React Native Reanimated, i18next
  (English and Spanish)
- **Backend**: Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth, Storage, Edge Functions on
  Deno, Realtime channels) with row-level security on every table
- **AI**: OpenAI GPT-4o for vision analysis of food, GPT-4o-mini for
  barcode clarification, OpenFoodFacts for product metadata
- **Monetization**: RevenueCat (StoreKit-side integration), three tiers
- **Video**: Remotion server-side for workout summary videos shareable
  to Instagram Stories
- **Build and submit**: EAS Build for cloud iOS and Android builds

## Architecture

```
Mobile (Expo + React Native, iOS + Android)
  └─> Supabase Edge Functions (Deno)
       ├─> analyze-food-v2     -> GPT-4o vision -> macros + health score
       ├─> analyze-barcode     -> OpenFoodFacts + GPT-4o-mini
       ├─> nutrition-service   -> macro lookup and normalization
       ├─> create-video-summary -> Remotion -> shareable video
       ├─> sync-subscription   -> RevenueCat webhook handler
       └─> 10 more functions   -> notifications, moderation, account ops
  └─> Supabase Postgres (RLS per table) + Storage + Realtime
  └─> RevenueCat (paywall, entitlements, trials)
```

The mobile app is the only client. Every external service goes through
an edge function so the client never holds third-party secrets.

## Key decisions

### Foundation models over training our own

Spotter does not train any vision model. GPT-4o is pretrained on a
nutrition and food corpus that no startup-sized team can reproduce,
and the cost per scan is a fraction of the engineering cost of getting
to comparable accuracy in-house. Where I needed to optimize cost, I
added cheaper paths (barcode lookup, manual entry with AI
normalization) rather than swapping the vision model.

### Supabase as the entire backend

Auth, Postgres, Storage, edge functions, real-time channels, all from
one provider. The alternative was four or five services stitched
together. The cost of operating Spotter at our current scale on
Supabase is in the tens of dollars per month, and the developer
ergonomics let one person own the whole backend.

### Cross-platform from day one

Expo + React Native shipped iOS and Android simultaneously instead of
the "iOS first, Android later" path most consumer apps take. Same
codebase, same release cycle, same feature parity. EAS Build handles
the native compilation in the cloud.

### Four pillars in one product, not four products

Most competitors do one thing — MyFitnessPal for nutrition, Strong for
workouts, Strava for the social layer. Spotter bundles them with a
shared identity, shared streaks, and a single feed. The product hook
is "your gym AND your meals AND your friends, in one place."

## Metrics

- **1,000+ downloads** in the first weeks on the App Store
- **350+ weekly active users**
- **Available on iOS and Android** in both Spanish and English
- **15 production edge functions** powering the backend
- **3-tier subscription** (Free, Pro, Premium) with a 7-day Pro trial
  on Pro features (advanced AI, LiDAR scan, extended analytics) and
  Premium adding coaching

## What I&apos;d do differently

I would have shipped an MVP v0.1 to production before chasing a v1.0
that felt complete. The version we launched in February could have
gone out in October with one pillar (nutrition) and grown from there.
The instinct to ship something polished cost us four months of
real-user feedback that no internal review can substitute.
