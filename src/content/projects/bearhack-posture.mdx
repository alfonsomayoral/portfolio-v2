## Overview

Built in 24 hours at the UC Riverside hackathon during my exchange
year (2024-25). Real-time webcam posture monitoring that alerts the
user through a small Arduino-based device when they slouch for too
long.

Won **3rd place** out of **40+ teams** in a hackathon with **150+
participants**.

## My role

Owned the computer vision pipeline: real-time pose estimation with
MediaPipe, processing the webcam feed to detect shoulder and neck
angles, and the integration between the Python detection process and
the Arduino feedback device over serial.

## Tech stack

- Python
- MediaPipe for pose estimation
- OpenCV for camera capture and preprocessing
- Arduino with custom firmware for the feedback hardware
- Serial communication between Python and Arduino

## What I learned

Hackathon scope discipline. We initially scoped a four-feature
product and nearly shipped nothing. Cutting back to one feature with
one excellent end-to-end demo — webcam to wrist alert — is what got
us on the podium.
